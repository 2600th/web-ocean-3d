How to read it. Integrated loudness is BS.1770-4 with both gates; true peak is 4x oversampled.
A click is a sample whose second difference stands more than
10x above both the 10 ms before and after it and above 0.004 absolute (first 0.5 s skipped). Voices are the
one-shot effects (against the tier cap) and music notes, sampled every 32 ms. Live nodes are
nodes created and not yet disconnected; nodes/s is the busiest second. Beds rendering at zero
are noise beds still connected while their level is zero. The repeat is when the score's
phrase order comes back to its start: measured if it happened in the render, else projected.
