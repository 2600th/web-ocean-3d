# Storylines

Three films for a one-minute social piece on Web Ocean 3D. The first is built.

## A. One day's voyage (built)

**Device and form:** a moment in a day, told as a flight through one world. The
viewer sails one day from a bright morning to a lantern-lit night, with a squall
in the afternoon, all in footage stepped out of the running game.

- **First two seconds:** the ship under full sail fills the right of the frame on
  turquoise water, already moving; "A ship" lands.
- **Beats:** ship → on deck with the crew → the player's helm view with the real
  interface → the reef under the surface → the island → the beach → golden hour →
  the squall and lightning (the payoff) → night under lanterns → the claim → the
  mark and the address.
- **Look:** the game's own picture, ungraded. Type is the game's own display face
  (Pirata One) in parchment white, bottom left, dead still once landed.
- **Sound:** the game's own score and sea, rendered offline by its audio engine;
  the storm arrangement arrives with the squall and thunder lands on the strike.

## B. Not a video

**Device and form:** the result first, then one frame that rebuilds. A storm
plays full screen; the camera pulls back to show it is a browser tab, and the
interface's sliders (wind, swell, hour) are dragged while the sea answers.

- **First two seconds:** lightning over a black sea; the line "Nothing here is a
  video" lands.
- **Beats:** storm → pull back to the tab → drag wind → drag hour → take the helm
  → mark.
- **Look:** the browser window on a deep sea-blue ground; the interface at twice
  size.
- **Sound:** storm bed dropping to room tone at the pull-back.

## C. Above and below

**Device and form:** match cuts across the waterline. Each shot above the surface
is answered by its counterpart below: hull and keel, reef and island, rain on the
surface seen from both sides.

- **First two seconds:** the lens half under water, the hull crossing it.
- **Beats:** waterline → keel → reef → shore → squall from below → night.
- **Look:** a hard horizontal split held across cuts.
- **Sound:** the mix ducks to the underwater arrangement on every dive.
